# Sky environment

`kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_2k.hdr` is the 2K HDR version of
[Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky),
by Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edits), distributed by Poly Haven
under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

Downloaded from Poly Haven's original HDR asset endpoint. The runtime loads
the local HDR file; it does not download assets or use a tonemapped JPG for
lighting. The supplied Niederwihl Forest files remain in `assets/` but are not
selected in the world configuration.
